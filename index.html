How to play

One idea, many ways to solve it. Every bounce, slide and roll is real physics.

Food falls

Droppers at the top rain ingredients down without stopping: fruit, milk and water.

Place tools

Drag kitchen tools into the sky and turn them. Every fruit that hits one changes course.

Machines cook

Steer each ingredient into the machine that needs it. The fruit you feed in decides the flavour.

Fill the order

Deliver every drink on the order before the clock hits zero. Move or take back any tool, any time.

Meet the chef

He runs the kitchen in the sky. He introduces every new food, cheers when an order is done, worries when the clock runs low, and celebrates every win with you.

Collect every recipe in his recipe book: a drink you have not made yet is a dark silhouette until the first time your kitchen makes it.
