Reporting a bug

Please include as much of this as you can, so we can find it quickly:

  • your phone model and Android version,
  • the game's version (in the Play Store, under "About this game"),
  • the level number and what you were doing,
  • a screenshot or a short screen recording, if you can.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your phone only. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your phone's settings starts it again from level 1.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Sky Kitchen plays fully offline. You only need a connection to watch an optional reward ad.
Do I have to watch ads?
Never. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward: free coins in the market, more time when the clock runs out, or a heart when you have none left. There are no banners and no ads that appear by themselves.
Can I buy anything?
No. There are no purchases in Sky Kitchen. Coins are earned by playing.
How do hearts work?
You have up to five hearts, and one comes back every 10 minutes. A heart is spent only when you give up a lost level, not when the clock runs out, because you are offered a continue first. A heart can also be bought with 150 coins, or for watching an ad when you have none left.
A level is too hard. Any tips?
Stop the clock and look: food freezes in mid-air, and you can build without pressure. Tap Analyze to watch the line at half speed. Every tool can be moved, turned or taken back for free, so try every idea. And if a level keeps beating you, tell us: we tune levels that too many players get stuck on.
Is it on iPhone?
Not yet. Sky Kitchen is on Android, on Google Play.

Your data

Sky Kitchen never asks who you are. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics, tied to a random number made on your phone, and the ads you choose to watch are served by Google AdMob. The full details are in the privacy policy.

  • To delete data already sent: email augames.info@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". Because the data is not tied to your name, please tell us the dates and the phone model you played on.
  • To start fresh: clearing the game's data in your phone's settings, or uninstalling it, deletes the random number. A new one is made the next time you play.
  • Ad choices: in the EEA and the UK, change your consent at any time in the game's Settings, under Privacy. Everywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings.
