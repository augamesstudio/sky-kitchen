Art

Bright low-poly 3D in a sky that never stops moving.

The look

Saturated colours on everything the player touches, a calm, low-saturation sky behind it, and no black shadows. Every model is low-poly and flat-shaded, coloured from one shared palette, so silhouette and colour read from far away and the whole game draws on a single material.

The game has no ground. Most factory games happen inside a building; this one hangs in the sky between the droppers above and the delivery bin below.

The chef

Rigged in 3D, with a face that changes with the level: his brows, eyes and mouth move with every mood.
